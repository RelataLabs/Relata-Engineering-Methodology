# RelataLabs and REM naming policy

The copyright licenses in this repository do **not** grant rights to RelataLabs branding or to present a modified work as an official RelataLabs publication.

This policy is intended to keep attribution and provenance clear while allowing broad reuse of REM.

## Permitted descriptive use

You may use the names **RelataLabs**, **Relata Engineering Methodology**, and **REM** when reasonably necessary to identify the origin of the work, describe compatibility, provide attribution, or explain that your work is based on REM.

Examples:

- `Based on Relata Engineering Methodology (REM) 1.0 by RelataLabs.`
- `An adaptation of REM 1.0 for Acme Corp.`
- `Compatible with the REM Plan format.`
- `Derived from REM 1.0; modified by Example Team.`

## Do not imply official status

Without written permission from RelataLabs, you may not use RelataLabs branding or official REM branding in a way that reasonably implies that a modified, forked, or independently maintained work is:

- an official RelataLabs release;
- endorsed, certified, or approved by RelataLabs;
- the canonical version of REM;
- a RelataLabs product or service.

A fork or adaptation should clearly identify that it has been modified when there is a reasonable risk of confusion.

## Names of modified versions

The licenses allow modification of the underlying licensed material. This naming policy asks maintainers of materially modified distributions to distinguish them from the canonical RelataLabs release.

Good examples:

- `REM 1.0 — Acme adaptation`
- `Acme Engineering Process, based on REM 1.0`
- `Fork of REM 1.0`

Avoid names or presentation such as `Official REM 2.0 by RelataLabs` unless RelataLabs actually published or authorized that release.

## Logos and visual identity

RelataLabs logos, marks, and distinctive visual branding are not included in the CC BY 4.0 or Apache-2.0 grants merely because they appear near licensed material.

Reasonable reproduction that is necessary for attribution or faithful archival reference is not intended to be restricted beyond applicable law, but reuse as branding for another product, organization, certification, or service requires separate permission.

## No registration claim

Nothing in this file is intended to state that any particular name or mark is registered in any jurisdiction. It only clarifies that the copyright licenses in this repository do not themselves grant branding rights.
